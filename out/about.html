How we work

AJKER খবর KI? explains the news for people who skip the newspaper. We don't do our own reporting. We read what trusted publishers report, explain it in plain words, and always send you back to them.

How we pick five stories

Every morning editors read the day's headlines from Bangladesh and around the world, then pick the five that matter most to your life here. Not the loudest five, and not an endless feed.

Every story has three layers

  1. ১

    The quick read

    What happened and who is involved, in about a minute.

  2. ২

    Why should I care?

    How it touches your money, studies, commute or family.

  3. ৩

    The full story

    Timeline, what we know, what's still developing, and every source.

Our standards

  • Every story names the original publisher and links their report
  • Summaries use only what the publisher reported: no added names, numbers or guesses
  • AI-written summaries are labelled, and can be wrong, so the source is always one tap away
  • We stay neutral on politics and never present speculation as fact
  • We never copy whole articles or publishers' photos. All pictures are our own drawings

Spotted a mistake?

Tap the button and tell us what looks wrong. When we correct a story, the correction is written on the story itself, with the date, so you can see what changed.
