# AJKER খবর KI?

News site for young people (about 10 to 40). Bangladesh and world news, explained in about 60 seconds. The name is always written exactly `AJKER খবর KI?`.

## How the code is laid out

- `components/SiteView.jsx`: the whole layout (header, home, story, all stories, about, footer). Converted from the approved Claude Design board "B. Rickshaw ride", so styles are inline text passed through `css()`.
- `components/Site.jsx`: all behaviour. `list()` is the story list (live headlines, today's five first), `renderVals()` builds what the view shows. Also holds the rickshaw game, dark mode, the kite page transition and the quiz.
- `lib/news.js`: reads the RSS feeds in `data/feeds.json` (cached 30 minutes) and picks a picture tag for each headline using `data/art-tags.json`.
- `lib/digest.js`: reads each main article's public text (`lib/article.js`) and asks an AI model (Gemini through `GEMINI_API_KEY`; other keys or Vercel AI Gateway are fallbacks, see `provider()`) to retell it in its own words: one 5 to 7 sentence overview, a one-line teaser, key points, "why care" and tricky words. **Each story's overview is saved on its own** (`overviewOf`, cache key `akk-ov-v2`, 3 days), so a story that appears an hour from now gets its overview at the next page refresh (10 to 40 minutes), not at the next 6-hour slot. A small batcher spreads the calls out and rests 5 minutes after a rate-limit failure. **A page never waits more than about 6 seconds for the AI** (article text, overviews and quiz each have a 6 s budget): what is not ready shows as the publisher's headline, and the unfinished work keeps running through `after()` so it is saved for the next refresh. Do not raise these budgets: a slow AI once made uncached pages take 40+ seconds. `getDigest()` is shared for 60 seconds per server instance, so a page's title and content (and pages built close together) fetch the news once. The 8-question quiz is written once per 6-hour slot (`extrasOf`, key `akk-extras-v3`). `getSimpler(id)` writes the "Explain it simpler" version on first tap and saves it (`akk-simple-v2`, English and Bangla kept apart). The Vercel data cache is shared by deployments, so **bump the cache key whenever a prompt or the saved shape changes**.
- `lib/today.js`: "Today in Dhaka" (weather and air quality from open-meteo, exchange rates for about 20 currencies from open.er-api.com, which updates once a day; readers pick any two currencies and an amount in the money converter, `lib/currencies.js`). No keys. Any part that fails is left out. `AKK_TODAY` serves fixed values offline.
- `app/api/simpler` (writes the easier retelling), `app/api/report` ("Report a mistake": readers' reports are written to the Vercel runtime log as `[report] {...}`, nothing else is stored), `app/api/health` (feed status), `app/api/news`.
- `components/Extras.jsx` + `app/extras.css`: the cha cup that fills as you read (every page) and the open sky (kites, clouds, birds, stars at night) beside the content on wide screens (shown when the window leaves at least 96px each side). The word of the day is brushed in by CSS in `globals.css` (`.ak-flap`).
- **Did you know** has its own page, `/did-you-know` (view `facts`), not on the home page. Ten facts a day, two each from Bangladesh, World, Science, Tech and Sport, all hand-written in `data/dyk/<section>.json` as `[hook, more, hookBn, moreBn]` (no AI, so they can be checked). The fact id comes from the English hook only, so editing the Bangla keeps "New to me" ticks and reports linked. `lib/dyk.js` `factsForDay()` walks each bank in one fixed shuffled order, so nothing repeats until the whole bank has been shown (the smallest bank sets how many days that is: add facts to make it longer). New ten at midnight Dhaka time. "New to me" ticks are counted on the reader's phone (`akk-learned`). Reports use kind `fact` with the fact id.
- `lib/dedupe.js`: drops headlines about the same event (used by `lib/news.js`). The dropped copies from other publishers are kept on the story as `also`, which the story page shows as "Also reported by".
- `lib/article.js`: fetches an article page and pulls out its paragraphs (not stored, not shown to readers).
- `data/stories.js`: built-in sample explainers, used only when no live news can be loaded.
- `lib/route.js`: web addresses (`/`, `/news/<id>`, `/story/<slug>`, `/stories`, `/did-you-know`, `/about`).
- `app/[[...path]]/page.jsx`: one page component serves every address, so page changes animate without a reload. Pages refresh every 10 minutes.
- Pictures are in `public/art/<tag>.svg` (day) and `<tag>-n.svg` (night). Share images are in `public/og` (`share.png`, a screenshot of the home page, is the preview for every page that is not a story).
- To work offline: `AKK_FIXTURE=path/to/digest.json npm run dev` serves a saved copy of `/api/news`.

## English and Bangla

- The **বাংলা / English** button in the header switches the whole site (saved on the phone as `akk-lang`; `?lang=bn` also works).
- Layout text: the page is written in English and `localize()` in `lib/i18n.js` swaps every piece of text found in its `BN` dictionary (plus aria-label, title, placeholder, alt). **When you add or change English text in SiteView.jsx, add its Bangla to `BN`**, or it stays English. Text built with numbers or names (times, counts, game messages) is made in Site.jsx with `B(en, bn)`.
- Stories and the quiz: the AI writes every story and question in both languages at once (`bn` field, see `STORY_PROMPT`, `quizPrompt`, `shapeBn`). A story whose Bangla is missing shows in English with a note. Headlines still waiting for the AI are the publisher's English titles.
- The Did you know facts carry their own Bangla (items 3 and 4 of each entry), shown when the site is in Bangla. Numbers use English digits; millions are given as কোটি/লাখ and centuries as শতক. A fact without Bangla falls back to English. Add both languages when adding a fact.

## Today's five

`pickFive()` in `lib/digest.js` chooses them with a fixed recipe from the live feeds (not by editors): the first 2 Bangladesh stories, the first World story, the first Sport story (else Tech), the first Tech story (else a second World story), then anything to reach five. Feeds are read in turns so no publisher fills the list, stories older than 3 days are dropped, and repeats of the same event are merged. On the home page the five are shown only in the "Today's five" section, which the game unlocks one passenger at a time, or "Open all five now" opens at once (remembered for the day in `akk-open`). "Live headlines" lists the stories after the five. "All stories" and search still list everything.

## Rules

- Keep the look: Anek Bangla font, the colour variables in `app/globals.css`, kite swoop transition, pictures switch with dark mode.
- There is no waitlist or email signup (removed on purpose: nothing would be sent to readers). Its place at the bottom of the home page is "Enjoying the ride?" (`#thanks`): tap to pour the maker a cha (cup animation in `app/extras.css`, `.th-*`), plus a link to the maker's Instagram. The header button "Say thanks" scrolls there. Under it, "Pay the fare, mama" (`.th-fare` in `app/extras.css`) shows the maker's bKash personal Send Money QR (`public/pay/bkash-qr.svg` on the page, `bkash-qr.png` for "Save QR"; never print the phone number on the site). Both were regenerated from the data decoded from the bKash card, black on white with the standard 4-module border (version 11, error correction M), because the card's own pink, denser code (version 15) was hard to scan from a screen; check any replacement decodes to the same data before shipping it plus a "Save QR" download for people on their phone. Real tips are optional and pay bKash's normal Send Money charge; say so wherever it is shown. On the 5th cup (counted in `akk-cha`, then reset to 0 when the note is closed; `akk-cha-od` remembers it happened) the page shakes and tears for 2 seconds with clicks blocked (`cg-on`, `.cg-tear` in app/extras.css; skipped with reduced motion), then the "Error 418: I'm a teapot, and I'm full." note covers the page (taps past 5 are ignored with a joke) until "Okay, water from now on". The Instagram link line is a joke too ("Tell the maker the cha worked", then "Tell the maker you are sorry" after 5 cups).
- Saved on the reader's own phone only: cha cups poured `akk-cha`, dark mode `akk-theme`, read stories `akk-read`, saved stories `akk-saved`, last visit `akk-visit` (and `akk-prev` in sessionStorage) for the "new since your last visit" count.
- Motion: shared curves are `--ak-out`, `--ak-io`, `--ak-drawer` in `app/globals.css` (section "Reader features and motion"). One-shot scroll-into-view moments use `data-io` on an element (`Site.arm()` adds `ak-armed` then `io-in`). Every animation has a gentler `prefers-reduced-motion` version.
- Summaries must be in our own words. Always name and link the original publisher. Never copy article text or publishers' photos. The overview must retell in new words, with no quotation marks.
- Never draw real, recognisable people, not even as cartoons, and never an avatar made for one named person. Pictures are scenes of the topic with no faces.
- A story's `tag` can carry more than the scene: `market|Tk 30/kg|bd,pk` means the market scene, a "Tk 30/kg" sticker, and the Bangladesh and Pakistan flags. `artOf()` in lib/news.js builds it from the headline using `factOf()` and `flagsOf()` in lib/scene.js, and `/api/art` (app/api/art/route.js) draws it on top of the file in `public/art`. `artFor()` in lib/route.js picks the URL.
- Stories about a death or tragedy (words like dies, killed, fatal, mourn) never get a cheerful picture (cricket, football, trophy, culture): they get `solemn`, a black ribbon and a candle. Extend the `SOMBER` list in lib/news.js if one slips through.
- `government`, `diplomacy` and `speech` show the Bangladesh flag, so `tagFor()` switches stories from World, Tech and Sport to the `-w` copies (plain blue banner). Edit both copies if you change one.
- The four "Explore by topic" cards carry a flag / world map / chip / sports picture (`TopicArt` in components/SiteView.jsx).
- **Phone app.** `android/` is a small native Android app (Java, one `MainActivity`): the live site in a full-screen WebView with no browser bar, a red splash with the kite, a bobbing-kite loading screen, pull to refresh, a bilingual no-internet screen with Retry, the back button moving back through pages, and other websites (publishers, WhatsApp, the APK download) opened outside the app. Package `app.vercel.ajker_khobor_ki.twa` and the same signing key as the first PWABuilder app, so it installs as an update over it.
  - **Building:** the workspace cannot reach the Android SDK, so `.github/workflows/android.yml` builds it on GitHub whenever `android/` changes and force-pushes the **unsigned** file to the `android-build` branch. Sign it here with `python3 scripts/sign_apk.py unsigned.apk signed.apk ~/ajker-signing/key.p12 ~/ajker-signing/.sp` (v2 signature; check with `python3 -m apksigtool verify`), then copy it to `public/ajker-khobor-ki.apk`.
  - **The key is NOT in the repo** (on Bishan's PC in the PWABuilder package zip in Downloads, and in the workspace under `~/ajker-signing`). A different key means phones refuse the update.
  - **To ship a new version:** raise `versionCode` in `android/app/build.gradle.kts`, `APP_VERSION` in `MainActivity.java` and `apkVersion` in `data/app.json` together, push, sign the new build, replace the file, deploy.
  - The site also works as an installable web app (`app/manifest.js`, icons in `public/icons`, `public/sw.js` with `public/offline.html`); `public/.well-known/assetlinks.json` ties the site to the app.
  - `components/InstallBanner.jsx` runs one bar for three jobs, all set in `data/app.json`: in a phone browser "Tap to download the app" (snoozed 14 days by `akk-app-no`); inside the app a "what's new" note once per `release.n` (remembered in `akk-rel`, **bump it whenever there is news for app users**); and "Download the update" when `apkVersion` is higher than the version the app opened with (the app opens the site with `?source=app&av=<version>`; the old PWABuilder app sends no `av`, which counts as 1).
- The rickshaw game draws a shorter, bigger stretch of road on phones (≤700px: `W = 560`, `.np3-cv`), and its messages sit under the road there instead of on top.
- The rickshaw game (`#game`) blocks text selection and the long-press copy menu, since it is played by holding buttons.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Must work on a 360px-wide phone.
